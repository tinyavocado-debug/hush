Numbers come from GoatCounter. It counts each page or event once per visitor in a short window, so someone who starts the same setup twice in a row counts once. Visitors are counted per page, so someone who opens the picker and then a room may count twice. Visitors with ad blockers aren't counted. Your own visits from localhost aren't counted either.
