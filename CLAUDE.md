# Hush

- **Start:** `python3 -m http.server 8766` from the repo root (no build step)
- **Port:** 8766
- **Main URL:** http://localhost:8766/ (rooms: `/orbit/`, `/ember/`, `/pond/`)
- **Usage stats:** http://localhost:8766/stats/ reads GoatCounter (tinyavocado.goatcounter.com) with a read-only API token kept in that browser. GoatCounter rate-limits its API (429), so the page spaces its calls, retries, and caches each range for 5 minutes. Events are sent from `shared/analytics.js` (`begin/<room>/<sound>/<breath>/<min>m`, `finished|ended-early/<room>/<min>m`); localhost visits aren't counted. Also shows time of day, systems, browsers, languages and campaigns (added 2026-11-19).
- **Journeys** (stats page, added 2026-11-19): one anonymous visit at a time (GoatCounter's session ID, same browser + connection for up to 8 hours; nobody is followed across days). "Load journeys" asks GoatCounter for a CSV export (once an hour, later ones only fetch newer rows; the token needs "Export" ticked) and keeps the rows in that browser; an export file can also be opened by hand. Needs "Individual pageviews" on in GoatCounter (Settings → Data collection). Shows a funnel, where visits ended, common paths and the latest visits.
- **Notes** (feedback), from `shared/feedback.js`: a mark in the top-right corner of every screen (in a session only with the controls, top left) opens a note that belongs to the room: **Turn over** on the picker (chosen 2026-11-19 over Dark window, a fourth unlit card, and Guestbook, one line under the rooms; the whole screen of rooms turns over like one card and the note is on its back, with optional Love it / Something's off / An idea tags and an optional room, which then files the note under that room), **Star** in Orbit (write a wish, hold, it flies up and stays in Orbit's sky; your own stars join into a constellation, kept in this browser), **Stones** in Pond (toss Love it / Something's off / An idea into the water, a lily pad comes up for optional words), **Smoke** in Ember (write a line, the letters drift off as smoke). Chosen 2026-11-19 from playable sketches (Lantern was turned into Star; Bubble, Quick and Edge were the plain trial before). The note goes to GoatCounter as the event `feedback/<where>/<text>` (anonymous, max 300 chars) and is listed under **Notes from visitors** on the stats page. From localhost the ceremony plays and says "Preview" instead of sending; if sending fails the note stays and says why. The picker footer link also opens it.

## Names

- **Hush**: the app. A room picker, then one room per scene.
- **Orbit** (was Still): deep space and the breathing dot sphere. Sounds: Float, Voyage, Silence. Since 2026-11-16 both are a relaxing fairytale among the stars, in D major, one chord per breath (D, Bm, G, A), with faint starlight and a deep hum: **Float** is a far-off choir (two long "ooh" notes a 4th or 5th apart through each breath out, the tune called **Beacon**) over solar wind and fairy dust (it began as the trial "Glade" with a wooden flute; the flute sounded too much like a garden), **Voyage** a storybook harp (the trial "Storybook"). Also tried that day: for Float's voice, the wooden flute, Glass, Theremin, Wonder (glass) and Swoop (theremin); Stardust, Nebula and Deep as more cosmic Floats; Music Box; Shruti, Vinyl, Purr; and the loving-kindness app's star-pad music (Kindness, Rise, Night). `ocean` is still in core.js, and Pond keeps `rain`.
- **Ember**: incense stick that burns down. Sounds: Drone (`temple` in code), Hearth, Silence.
- **Pond**: a koi pond seen from above (lily pads, lotus, three koi). Was **Cairn** (stones at a lake) until 2026-11-07. Sounds: Garden, Rain, Silence.
- **Modes**: on every room's setup screen, above the sounds, **Sleep / Steady** as two **orbs**, each breathing at its own mode's rhythm (added 2026-11-16; Orbs chosen the same day over a pill row, a swipeable big word, and a moon dragged along a rail). **Sleep** breathes at the Long pace and ends with no bells and no vibration (its sound fades over about 20s). **Steady** opens the **breath menu** pop-up with Short / Steady / Long plus **Hold**; a pill under the orbs shows the choice and reopens it. The third orb, **Breath** (the twelve one-word techniques, `Spa.TECHNIQUES` in `shared/core.js`), was removed 2026-11-27: the techniques are now reached through the **Program**. The mode, pace and program are shared by all rooms and remembered; first visit starts Steady.
- **Program** (`shared/program.js`, added 2026-11-27, chosen from sketches at `/sketches/program.html` over **Moon**, a 28-night course, and **Lotus**, petals that open by feel): the twelve techniques as a row of **stars** in a small framed sky at the top of every room's setup screen, between the back arrow and the notes mark, climbing from Beginner (Balance, Alertness, Recovery, Body) through Intermediate (Movement, Senses, Concentration, Stillness) to Expert (Control, Discipline, Activating, Advanced). Tapping a lit or current star picks it (the Program mode): its breath, and its length on the dial (5, 10 or 15 minutes; placeholders). After a program session of at least a minute the complete screen asks **How was that?** **Easy** lights the star and opens the next (expert stars need two sessions first). **About right** adds five minutes. **Hard** eases the next one (holds halved, a long breath in or out shortened, five minutes less). **Every session fills a dot whatever the answer, and three dots light a star**, so there is always progress (Ingrid: no progress after Hard would be discouraging). A star lit while eased opens the next one eased. Answers go to GoatCounter as `program/<technique>/<easy|right|hard>` (not yet on the stats page). Kept in this browser.

## Settled

- The picker's background is **Lanterns** (chosen 2026-11-19, replacing the old brown): a quiet midnight blue-black, and each room's card glows in its own colour (violet, amber, teal) like a lit window, the glows breathing slowly out of step. The rule: the background stays neutral and the cards bring the colour. Also tried: Aurora, Dusk, Inkwash, Fireflies, Opal and Jellies (they brought their own colours, which fought the cards), Thread (a twilight running indigo, wine, teal under the cards) and Moonstone (pale grey, each card casting a coloured shadow).

- Every soundscape follows the visuals' breath curve (`breathCurve` in `shared/core.js`), so sound and scene rise and settle together.
- Ember's Drone uses **cues**: a high chime as each breath in starts, a low one as each breath out starts, over a gentle bloom of upper tones and air. (Also tried: a wider swing, and separate in/out textures.)
- Ember's stick: the original tip and straight ash, plus a few **sparks** rising from the tip as each breath in starts (with the high chime), and the ash dropping as each breath out starts, so the smoke gives a small jump. Also tried and dropped: smoke rings on each breath out, stirring the smoke by touch, and a glowing coal with curling ash.
- Pond's scene is the **koi pond** (chosen 2026-11-07; it replaced Cairn's stone stack at a lake, and the room was renamed Pond). A ring spreads from the centre as each breath in starts; rain drops ring the water; each garden chime drops a petal. Also tried: a rainy window that fogs on the breath out, and a bamboo grove that sways.
- Orbit's look: the picker card is a violet-blue glow on near-black with twinkling stars. The room ("Glow") is a high-contrast violet glow behind the dial fading fast to near-black, with glimmering stars and a faint haze; the sphere keeps its warm stone-and-gold dots, and the Begin button and dial arc stay cream. Also tried: Moon, Spring, Stone and a light baby-blue Sky as other names and palettes; a spiral galaxy behind the dial; and Drift, Blend (Drift with a blue halo round the sphere), Blend Dark, Nebula (a pink-white bloom with sparkle stars and streaks), Halo (near-black with a blue halo) and Ultraviolet room styles.
